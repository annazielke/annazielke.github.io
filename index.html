About

Hi! I am a Senior ML Scientist at Centile Bio. I have a PhD (cum laude) in AI in Medical Imaging from Maastricht University, GROW - Research Institute for Oncology and Reproduction.

Previously, I was a Post Doctoral Researcher at AIM lab - Harvard MGB and KannLab and Editorial Board Trainee at Radiology: AI, Tech Consultant in AI&DL at Amazon Web Services (AWS) and an AI fellow at C10 Lab in Boston, MA. I did my master's in Biomedical Computing at TU Munich. I spent a year as a Visiting Scholar at Johns Hopkins University in ARCADE lab; worked as a Research Assistant at MITI in Klinikum rechts der Isar; worked at ScintHealth (IFL Lab), and took part in the MedInnovate Graduate Program where VizCare was born. I did my Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering at NTUU "KPI".

My special interest is medical imaging applications and a variety of computational methodologies around it, such as computer vision, machine learning, image processing, and growth modeling.
